InviteMagic Privacy Policy

Effective 30 October 2026 · App: InviteMagic (package app.invitemagic)

InviteMagic helps you design wedding, birthday and housewarming invitations and share them with guests. This policy explains what the app stores, where, and why. In short: your designs and photos stay on your phone, and only the parts you choose to share leave it.

1. What stays on your phone

Your drafts, names, dates, venues, photos and the design you make are stored on your device only. They are never uploaded to our servers. Exporting a PNG, PDF or MP4 happens on your phone.

2. What is sent when you share a guest link

If you create a hosted invitation link, we send the invitation text and details you entered (names, date, venue, template) to our server so guests can open it. Guest photos are not uploaded; photos are not supported on hosted pages.

When a guest replies, we store their name, attending status, number of guests and any note they write. The owner of the link can see these replies, and no one else can.

3. Sign-in

You can sign in with your Google account through Firebase Authentication. The app receives a sign-in token, which our server checks. We store only an anonymous identifier derived from it. We do not store your name, email address or profile picture in our database.

4. Purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. Our server checks each purchase with Google Play and records only the plan and its validity period. If you request a refund through Google, your plan is removed.

5. Notifications

If you turn on notifications, your device's push token is stored with Firebase Cloud Messaging so we can send you a notification when a guest replies. You can turn this off in the app at any time.

6. Spam protection

Guest replies are protected by Cloudflare Turnstile, a bot check. It does not show a visible puzzle in most cases.

7. How long we keep data

  • Hosted invitations expire 90 days after the event date and are then deleted, together with their guest replies.
  • If you stop sharing a link, its guest replies and tokens are deleted immediately.
  • Daily backups of hosted invitations are kept in private storage for 14 days.

9. Your choices

You can delete your local data from the app's settings. You can stop sharing any invitation, which deletes its hosted data immediately. To ask for deletion of data we hold, email us.

10. Children

InviteMagic is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

11. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and this page.
